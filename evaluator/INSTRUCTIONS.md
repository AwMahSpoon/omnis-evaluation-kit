# Instructions for External Evaluator

## What you receive

- Frozen criteria from `criteria/`
- Raw data from `raw-data/`
- Mechanical rules from `scoring/`

## What you must not receive

- The system's own analysis, conclusions, or reasoning
- Prior self-assessment scores
- Any document explaining why a result should pass

## Your job

Apply the mechanical rules. Report the score. Nothing else.

## If you are an AI evaluator

State your model name and version. Do not use tools that could reach the system's other artifacts. Score from the files provided only.

## If you are a human evaluator

No domain expertise required. The rules are written to be applied mechanically. If a rule is ambiguous, mark the round UNSCORED and note the ambiguity.
